The Company

One Life.

The quiet force behind the apps you use to live better.

One Life is a holding company for daily life apps. We sit quietly above the products people love, like the calm hand behind your favourite things. You see and use the apps. You may never think about us. That is the point.

Why we exist

Modern life got loud. Too many apps, too much noise, too little calm. We build a small family of focused tools that each do one thing beautifully, so the simple parts of life feel simple again. Less friction, less noise, more of the life you actually want.

How we build

One app, one job, done with care. We design for restraint rather than engagement. We do not chase your attention or sell your data. We measure success by how little you have to think about us, and how much better your day feels because the apps are there.

Our apps
