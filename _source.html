Just Press Play

The right sound for how you feel.

Not guided meditation. Functional, designed sound that meets the moment you are in. Five needs, one tap away, with a real soft spot for ADHD and busy minds.

Sleep

Brown noise, rain, ocean and low steady beds for the middle of the night, plus world lullabies, a sleep timer and a real sleep score from Apple Health.

Focus

Steady study music and focus sound with no lyrics, from cafe and library ambience to pink noise, with a one-tap focus timer to protect the session.

Stress

Slow, calming sound for stressful days, from ocean and forest to soft ambient, plus simple guided breathing to bring you back down.

ADHD

Steady, low-surprise sound made to help you settle, tune out distraction and stay on task, including brown noise and white noise, with a Pomodoro-style timer. Built with ADHD minds in mind.

Kids

Gentle lullabies, white noise and soothing bedtime sounds for little ones, with an auto-stop timer and a built-in safe-volume reminder.

How It Works

How Luten works.

Four steps, and the first two take a few seconds.

1. Say how you feel
Pick Sleep, Focus, Stress, ADHD or Kids, or tell Sona in your own words, such as tired, wired or cannot focus.
2. Press play
Luten plays instrumental sound matched to that need, crossfading between tracks so it never sounds like one file on loop.
3. Put the phone down
Add a sleep timer that fades the sound out, or a focus timer. Playback continues with the screen locked, and downloaded sounds play offline.
4. It learns what you keep
Luten notices which sounds you keep playing and suggests those first, on your device.

Why It Feels Different

Not another meditation app.

No voice to follow, nothing to get right. You press play, and the sound does the work.

Functional sound

Utility sound for a job, sleep, focus or calm, not a meditation course.

World soundscapes

Cultural and world sound worlds you will not hear anywhere else, designed by intent rather than picked at random.

Made for ADHD

Built with ADHD minds in mind, a quiet anchor for restless, easily distracted attention.

No pressure

It stays in the wellness lane, relaxation, focus and rest, with no homework and no streak guilt.

Luten or a Playlist

When Luten is the right choice.

A music playlist and Luten both put sound in your ears. They are built for different jobs.

Choose a playlist
When you want songs you know, lyrics, a favourite artist, or music to enjoy for its own sake.
Choose Luten
When you want sound for one job, sleep, focus, stress, ADHD or a child's bedtime, chosen by how you feel rather than by searching.
What plays
A playlist plays whatever tracks it holds, which can include vocals and changes in volume. In Luten every sound is instrumental, with even loudness and crossfaded loops.
Built-in tools
Luten adds a sleep timer that fades out, focus and Pomodoro-style timers, guided breathing, and a sleep score from Apple Health.

The Signature

Two things you feel right away.

The heart of Luten: Sona, who learns what to play for you, and a sleep score that tells you the truth.

Sona, your sound companion

Tell Sona you are tired, wired, anxious or cannot focus. She listens, meets you where you are, and recommends a real track by name, then learns what actually settles you. She runs entirely on your device, so nothing you type ever leaves your phone.

Real sleep score

An honest 0 to 100 score built from your Apple Health sleep, with a full breakdown of duration, deep, REM and interruptions, plus vitals like heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate and blood oxygen. No vanity numbers.

What's Inside

More than a play button.

A considered set of tools that work together, quietly.

Adaptive soundscapes

An evolving mix that crossfades between tracks across a session, not one file on loop.

A player that just breathes

No menus to read at 3am. The Luten mark turns slowly and breathes with whatever is playing, on a black screen that stays dark at night.

Always-on playback

Background play, offline downloads, looping and continuous routines that keep going instead of stopping after one track.

Gentle reminders

Wind-down and daily ritual nudges, plus a soft come-back reminder. Calm prompts, never a jarring alarm.

Streaks & 7-Day Starter

A short starter program and gentle streaks to build a calming habit, without the guilt.

Widget, Watch & stickers

A home-screen widget, an Apple Watch companion and iMessage stickers.

Family profiles

Two to four members, each with their own space. Your profile syncs to the cloud and survives a reinstall.

Design

An Apple Liquid Glass interface over a living sky that moves with your day: blue at noon, purple at dusk, deep violet at night, with the sun and moon arcing overhead. Premium, quiet, low pressure.

What's Inside

More than a record button.

Small things that make a long recording easy to use afterwards.

Mark the moment

Questions

Questions, answered.

Is Cassette free?

Yes, to download and to record, with no minute meter. Transcripts and notes are free for 5 tapes a month. Cassette Premium writes up every tape and backs up your shelf, and the annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

Does my audio leave my iPhone?

The audio stays on your iPhone unless you sign in with Premium to back it up. Transcripts come from Apple speech recognition, on the device wherever your iPhone supports your language; otherwise that recording may be sent to Apple to transcribe. The notes are written by Apple's models, never by an outside AI service: on iOS 27 the transcript goes to Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which does not keep it, and on earlier iPhones the notes are written on the device.

Do I need an account?

No. Recording, transcripts and notes all work without one. Signing in keeps your purchase with you, and with Premium it backs up every tape so it comes back on a new iPhone.

What goes in the notes?

A short summary, the highlights worth remembering, and the action items with owners and due dates when they are mentioned, plus a follow-up email draft for the people who came up.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone on iOS 17 or later. Notes by Apple Intelligence need an iPhone that supports it; other iPhones get simpler notes, written on the device.

Can I share what it writes?

Yes. Copy a whole tab in one tap, or save the email, the notes or the transcript as a PDF or a Word document.

Does it keep recording with the screen off?

Yes. Lock your iPhone or switch apps and the tape keeps rolling. A phone call pauses it, and you pick up where you left off.

How do I delete my recordings?

Open a tape and press Delete, or press and hold it on the shelf. Deleting your account removes every backed-up recording, transcript and note too.

Luten

Luten is an iOS app of sounds for sleep, focus and calm, with Sona, a companion you can chat with, plus Home Screen widgets, an Apple Watch app and iMessage stickers. You can use Luten without an account. Your listening history, streaks, downloads, sound settings, profile photo and Sona conversations stay on your iPhone.

Luten accounts

An account is optional. You can sign in with Sign in with Apple, Google, or an email address and password, through Google Firebase Authentication. We keep your name, email address (or the private relay address Apple gives us if you choose to hide yours), account ID and sign-in method. Without an account, Luten gives your install a random ID that is not linked to your name or email. If you create an account later, the data on that install moves to your account.

Luten profile backup

When you are signed in, Luten backs up your profile so it can be restored if you reinstall or change phones: your name and email address, the section you chose, your bedtime and focus time, your reminder settings and how many days a week you want reminders, your age range and gender (both hold a default value until you change them), your plan and trial dates, a phone number if you add one, and whether notifications and Apple Health access are turned on. Luten 1.3.0 and earlier also store a referral code linked to your account ID and email address; later versions do not, and deleting your account removes any that exists. Your listening history, streaks, downloads, profile photo and Sona conversations are not backed up.

Luten and Sona

Sona runs on your iPhone, using Luten's own rules and Apple's on-device language technology. It does not use an outside AI service. What you type to Sona is never uploaded or stored, and the conversation is gone when you leave it. Each time Sona replies, Luten records a usage event with the section it matched, a broad label for the kind of message (for example a feeling or a question about the app), whether Sona understood it, and how many sounds it suggested. Nothing is recorded when a message suggests someone may be in crisis or at risk of harm, or, in versions after Luten 1.3.0, when it is about a health, medical or eating topic. Luten 1.3.0 and earlier include those labels. Sona is a wellness companion, not a medical or crisis service.

Luten and Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Luten, it asks permission to read your sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen and step count. Improve Recommendations, in Profile under Privacy, is off until you turn it on. Luten usage analytics

To understand how Luten is used, it sends usage events to PostHog, on servers in the United States. With each event PostHog receives your IP address, an approximate location it works out from that address (country, region, city and postal code), the app version, and your device type and screen size. Events are linked to your install's random ID and, once you sign in, to your account ID, and events from before you signed in are joined to your account. Luten uses Sentry, on servers in the United States, for crash reports and performance data: what went wrong, the app version, device and system details, and the steps that led up to it. Reports are linked to your account ID when you are signed in, and in Luten 1.3.0 and earlier to your email address as well.
